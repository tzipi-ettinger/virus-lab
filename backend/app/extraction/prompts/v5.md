You are reading ONE scanned page of a Hebrew medical "commitment form" (טופס התחייבות)
received by the Central Virology Laboratory. Extract the fields below.

Fields:
- form_type, the family of the form:
  hmo_form = a health fund's own form (כללית, מכבי, מאוחדת, לאומית);
  hospital_form = a hospital's own commitment or external-test request form;
  hospital_system_screenshot = a printout of a hospital computer screen (tables, toolbar
  icons, fields such as "גורם מפנה" and "קשרי ביטוח");
  lab_report = a printed lab-system report or results page (e.g. LabOS);
  private_lab = a private lab's referral form;
  government = IDF / Ministry of Defense / National Center of Forensic Medicine;
  unknown = none of these.
- first_name, last_name: the PATIENT's name only (labels such as "שם", "שם פרטי",
  "שם משפחה", "שם הנבדק", "שם החולה"). Names next to "אב:" (father), "אם:" (mother),
  a doctor, a signature or a clerk are NOT the patient's name.
- national_id: the patient's ID, labeled "ת.ז.", "ת"ז", "מספר זהות", "ID / PASS".
  On a printout of a hospital computer screen the patient's ID is the number at the top,
  next to the patient's name and birth date; the number under "מקרה" below it is the
  case number, never the ID. An ID printed next to the name of whoever printed or
  issued the form (e.g. "הודפס ע"י ... תעודת זהות") belongs to that clerk, not to the
  patient.
- central_numbers: the lab's internal number. Usually a sticker with a barcode and the
  word "מרכזי" printed next to it. It can also be handwritten (often a 6-digit number
  with a date written next to or under it), including on printouts of a hospital
  computer screen. One form may carry several central-number stickers (one per sample
  tube): return all of them. Stickers or barcodes from other labs or hospitals are NOT
  central numbers.
- received_date: the date the lab received the sample. Usually inside a stamp "התקבל"
  (המעבדה המרכזית לנגיפים), or handwritten directly next to / under the central number.
  When the page has a "התקבל" stamp with a date, use that date, even if another date is
  written next to the central number. NOT the birth date ("ת.לידה", the date next to
  the patient's name on a screen printout), print date, visit date or sample
  collection date.
  The year: these forms are recent, and in handwriting and faded stamps a "6" is easily
  read as "0" or "3". Before returning a year that differs from the other dates printed
  on the same page (print, visit, request dates), look at that digit again. If you still
  cannot tell, status="unclear" with your reading in evidence.
- sender: the organization that ISSUED the commitment and pays the lab:
  * a health fund's form -> that fund;
  * a hospital's own form -> that hospital, even if the hospital belongs to a health
    fund's network, shows a fund's logo, or the patient belongs to another fund
    (e.g. a Kaplan form with a Clalit logo that says "נא לחייב את חשבון בית החולים" -> קפלן);
  * a printout of a hospital computer screen -> the health fund in the "גורם מפנה" field;
  * a private lab's form -> that lab.
  When you recognize the organization, return its name exactly as written in this list:
  {senders}
  Otherwise copy the name as written on the page.
- commitment_numbers: payment commitment numbers. Labels include "מספר התחייבות",
  "התחייבות", "דרישה", "דגימה" / "מספר דגימה" (Leumit, and forms that say the sample
  number serves as the commitment number), "אסמכתא", and the "מספר התחייבות" column in
  an insurance table. On Wolfson (וולפסון) forms the commitment number is the number on
  the vertical barcode sticker. Some hospitals print it with no label at all, on a
  barcode sticker next to the hospital's name or logo at the top, or on a vertical
  sticker at the page's edge (e.g. "436702", "2648 00879", "82857"): when the form is a
  commitment form and such a sticker is not a central number, a patient sticker or
  another lab's sticker, it is the commitment number. A commitment number may start
  with a letter (e.g. "X132767"): copy the letter too. The central number is NEVER also
  the commitment number. Some forms (e.g. private lab referrals) have no
  commitment number at all: then return an empty list, never another number instead.
  Return each distinct commitment number once, even if it is printed several times.

Numbers that are NEVER the national_id and NEVER a commitment number:
"מקרה" / "מס' מקרה" (case number), "מס' ביקור" and visit numbers with a suffix such as
"2825747366-01", "מספר רישיון", "ת.דרישה", a printed
form serial number such as "מס' 58153", phone and fax numbers, CPT / test codes
("קוד"), and numbers on stickers of other labs.

Rules:
- Never guess or complete a value. If a field is absent, status="not_found", value=null.
- If a value is present but you cannot read it with certainty, status="unclear" and put
  your best partial reading in evidence, value=null.
- status="not_found" always goes with value=null. If you return a value, its status is
  "found".
- Copy identifiers exactly as written, keep leading zeros. If a number is written with a
  prefix separated by "/" (e.g. "606/50364186"), copy it exactly including the "/"
  (the code removes known lab-code prefixes).
- Dates as written on the page (do not reformat).
- evidence: the label printed next to the value and where on the page it appears.
- The page may be rotated; report page_rotation_degrees = clockwise rotation (0, 90, 180
  or 270) needed to make the text upright.
